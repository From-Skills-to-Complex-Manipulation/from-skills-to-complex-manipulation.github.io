Privacy Policy

This website only displays information about the workshop. It has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising, and we do not collect personal data from visitors.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Who runs this site

This site is run by the organizers of the workshop “From Skills to Complex Manipulation: Grounding, Learning and Control” at ICRA 2027. It is not an official IEEE or ICRA website. You can reach us through the contact details on the Organizers page.

What we collect

Nothing. The site has no sign-up, no contact or submission forms, no comments and no analytics or tracking scripts.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit, your browser sends GitHub the usual technical information needed to deliver a web page, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. GitHub may keep this in server logs to operate and secure its service. We do not have access to those logs. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not set cookies and do not store anything in your browser.

Fonts, images and external content

All fonts, images and scripts are served from this site. We do not embed content from social networks, video platforms, map services or other third parties. Links to other websites, such as the ICRA 2027 site, take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Speakers and organizers

Names, affiliations, photos and short biographies of speakers, panelists and organizers are published with their consent. If you are listed and want something corrected or removed, contact us and we will update the site.

Paper submissions

Papers are submitted through an external submission platform, not through this website. That platform’s own privacy policy applies to any data you give it. Accepted papers are posted here only with the authors’ permission.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example in the EU, EEA or UK, you have rights over your personal data, including access, correction and deletion. Since this site does not collect visitor data, there is usually nothing for us to provide or delete. For data held by GitHub, please contact GitHub directly.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

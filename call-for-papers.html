Submissions

Call for Papers

We invite short papers and extended abstracts on any part of the path from individual manipulation skills to complex, long-horizon and contact-rich tasks. Work in progress, negative results and system papers are all welcome.

Deadline March 2027Non-archivalICRA conference format

Topics

Topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Skill discovery, representation and skill libraries
  • Composing and sequencing skills for long-horizon tasks
  • Task and motion planning with learned skills
  • Vision-language-action and other foundation models for manipulation
  • Grounding language, goals and instructions in perception
  • Affordances and object-centric representations
  • Imitation learning from demonstrations, video and play
  • Reinforcement learning and sim-to-real transfer
  • Contact-rich and dexterous manipulation
  • Force, impedance and whole-body control
  • Bimanual and mobile manipulation
  • Failure detection, recovery and safe execution
  • Benchmarks, datasets and evaluation for long-horizon manipulation

Submission formats

Up to 4 pages + references

Short paper

Complete but early work: new methods, systems, datasets or evaluations. Appendices are allowed, but reviewers are not required to read them.

Up to 2 pages + references

Extended abstract

Late-breaking results, work in progress, demos and position statements that will start a good discussion.

Formatting and review

  • Use the ICRA conference format (two columns, US Letter), the same format as papers in the ICRA main track.
  • Submit a single PDF. Links to supplementary videos or code are welcome in the paper.
  • Reviewing is single-blind, so include author names and affiliations.
  • Each submission receives at least two reviews from the program committee. We look at relevance, clarity and how much discussion the work is likely to start.

Presentation at the workshop

  • Accepted papers are introduced in the contributed poster teaser session (11:25–11:45) and presented in the poster session that follows (11:45–12:30).
  • At least one author of each accepted paper must register for the workshop and present in person.

Archival policy and dual submission

The workshop is non-archival. With the authors’ permission, accepted papers are posted on this website, but they are not formal publications and can later be submitted to conferences or journals. Work under review elsewhere is welcome as long as the other venue’s rules allow it.

Awards

We plan to present a Best Paper Award and a Best Poster Award at the end of the day. Details will follow once sponsorship is confirmed.

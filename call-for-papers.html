Call for Papers

Deadline March 2027Non-archivalICRA conference format

Topics

Topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Unimanual or bimanual mobile object manipulation
  • Non-prehensile object manipulation
  • Simple grasping and manipulation with external dexterity
  • Manipulation in clutter
  • Manipulation in constrained spaces
  • Shelf replenishment
  • Large object manipulation
  • Manipulation planning
  • Skill composition and long-horizon reasoning
  • Multimodal perception and grounding

Submission format

Short papers of up to 4 pages plus references.

Formatting and review

  • Use the ICRA conference format (two columns, US Letter), the same format as papers in the ICRA main track.
  • Submit a single PDF. Links to supplementary videos or code are welcome in the paper.
  • Reviewing is single-blind, so include author names and affiliations.
  • Each submission receives at least two reviews from the program committee.

Presentation at the workshop

  • Accepted papers are introduced in the contributed poster teaser session (11:25–11:45) and presented in the poster session that follows (11:45–12:30).
  • At least one author of each accepted paper must register for the workshop and present in person.

Archival policy and dual submission

The workshop is non-archival. With the authors’ permission, accepted papers are posted on this website, but they are not formal publications and can later be submitted to conferences or journals. Work under review elsewhere is welcome as long as the other venue’s rules allow it.

Awards

We plan to present a Best Poster Award in the awards session (16:25–16:35).

Panel discussion

16:15–17:15 · In the program

What stands between today’s skills and tomorrow’s tasks?

The panel brings together speakers from grounding, learning and control, plus a view from industry, to argue about where the field should put its effort next. Some of the questions we plan to put to them:

  1. Do large pretrained policies make explicit skill libraries obsolete, or more important than ever?
  2. Where should structure come from: planners, learned latent spaces or language?
  3. What would a fair benchmark for long-horizon, contact-rich manipulation look like?
  4. What will it take to run multi-step manipulation reliably outside the lab?

Panel discussion

16:35–17:45 · In the program

Panel topic to be announced

The invited speakers join the panel. The discussion covers the themes and questions the workshop sets out to address:

  1. Should complex manipulation be built through reusable skills and explicit composition?
  2. Can end-to-end learned policies or VLA models acquire this composition implicitly?
  3. What role should world models, hierarchical policies, multimodal grounding and feedback play?
  4. How do we maintain robustness and reactivity when moving from demonstrations in controlled settings to genuinely dynamic environments?
From Skills to Complex Manipulation

Grounding, Learning and Control. A full-day workshop at ICRA 2027, the IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation. Friday, 28 May 2027, Coex, Seoul.
